About Daula Attire

Daula Attire is a house of luxury menswear built on one belief: a garment should be made for the man, not the other way round. Every piece begins with a conversation and a careful set of measurements.

From there, our master tailors draft an individual pattern, select cloth from fine mills and shape each panel by hand. Fittings refine the silhouette until the drape, balance and comfort are exactly right.

The result is clothing with quiet confidence — suits, sherwanis and formal wear that carry our name and your character.

Why Choose Daula Attire

Three generations of craft knowledge guide every stitch. We work with premium wool, silk and jamawar, and every garment is cut on our own premises in Karachi.

Your pattern is kept on file, so future orders keep the same impeccable fit. Wedding parties, corporate wardrobes and single statement pieces receive the same attention to detail.

We finish what we start: buttonholes, linings and embroidery are completed in-house and inspected before every handover.
